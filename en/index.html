What I do

From the first idea to a finished app

I handle the whole journey myself – and I like to do things properly.

  • Apps for Android

    I build the whole app – from the first screen to a finished version that's ready for Google Play.

  • Game design

    Levels, bosses, weapons and balance that make you want one more round.

  • Art and visual style

    Logo, icons, characters and store images in one consistent style.

  • Sound and voices

    Music, sound effects and voice lines I recorded myself.

  • Many languages

    Apps that more people can use. My first game is available in 11 languages.

  • Testing and release

    Closed testing with real users, a privacy policy and Google Play's rules in order.

  • AI as a partner

    I work with modern AI tools for code, art and music. The idea, the design and the final choices are mine.

How I work

Small steps, high quality

  1. Idea

    It starts with a simple thought: what would be fun?

  2. Design

    I sketch how it should look and feel.

  3. Build and test

    I build, try it out and fix things – again and again.

  4. Release and improve

    The app goes out, and I listen to the people who use it.

About me

Hi, I'm Emil

Emil Studio is my own small indie studio in Denmark. I make games and apps that I'd want to use myself – and I care about the details: that it feels good, looks good and is fair to the people using it.

I enjoy the whole journey: coming up with the idea, drawing, building, testing and finally pressing “Publish”. My first big project, Kingdom Defense: Sealed Worlds, is in closed testing and coming soon to Google Play.

– Emil

My rules for fair play
